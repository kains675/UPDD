## PATH Diagnosis

1. W23A s101 has a direction/leg-specific structural signal.

`w23a_s101_bound_dminus` shows much more close water/site contact than the
s101 dplus and free probes: frac<0.26 is 0.293 versus 0.043 for bound dplus and
0.032 for free dplus. This supports the tail-mechanism suspicion for the
bound/dminus high-leverage cell.

2. W23A is not seed-unique.

The bound/dminus control `w23a_ctrl_s42_bound_dminus` also has a non-trivial
frac<0.26 of 0.180. Therefore the observed contact is not purely an s101
idiosyncrasy. The safer interpretation is: bound/dminus has a setup-level
water/site penetration tendency, with s101 amplifying it.

3. W4A free/dplus is not a high-leverage-specific differentiator.

`w4a_carved_s127_free_dplus` and its free/dplus control are essentially matched
for close-contact fraction: 0.167 versus 0.175. The free/dplus contact signal is
therefore not a s127-specific tail explanation.

4. W4A carved bound/dminus has the strongest close-contact fraction, but the
control is also high.

`w4a_carved_s127_bound_dminus` has frac<0.26 of 0.515, compared with 0.275 for
`w4a_carved_ctrl_s163_bound_dminus`. This is an amplification of the high-
leverage cell, not a clean on/off distinction. It points to a carved
bound/dminus structural pathology that is broader than one seed.

5. Treat very small minima as alarm flags, not calibrated distances.

The sub-0.05 nm minima in several bound/dminus cells are strong pathology
flags. The more reliable discriminator is the distributional fraction
frac<0.26 and p50, not the single minimum. Lack of PBC correction in the simple
DCD distance pass should not create false too-small Euclidean distances, but
copy identity and local-water selection still make these values diagnostic
rather than quantitative structural measurements.

## Decision Implication

D2 does not justify abandoning the prior B-first plan. It does justify adding a
tail-mechanism structural gate to the B densify + seed expansion campaign.

Recommended ordering:

1. Continue D2/B: densify + seed expansion, but carry DCD-on structural probes
